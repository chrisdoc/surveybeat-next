# Being an Organization admin grants no access to any Team's Results

The Organization admin role (WorkOS's org-level `admin`) covers administration only: Teams, Leads, and People. Seeing a Team's Results, comments, or Actions requires a Membership in that Team, whatever the person's Organization role. Admins are often the people members worry about most. If the admin role could read every Team's Results, it would undercut "trust before features" and put leadership roll-ups (Release 3) in through a side door.

> Extended by ADR 0014 (an Organization admin may add themselves to a Team).
