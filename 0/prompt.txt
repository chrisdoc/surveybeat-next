## Scope (summary; #38 is binding)
- An Organization admin can create and rename Teams, assign and remove Leads, and add and remove People. **Being an admin gives no access to any Team's Results** (ADR 0003).
- A Lead or admin adds members by email. That creates a Person (email only, no WorkOS user yet) and a Membership. It sends "You've been added to <Team> by <Lead>" through the slice-05 EmailSender and send states: captured in D1 on non-production stages, not sent.
- Rules:
  - the last Lead can't be removed;
  - removing a member ends their Membership on that date, and the Person and their history stay;
  - Teams don't nest;
  - the roles are lead and member (viewer is modelled but unused).
- Removing a Person:
  - keep the D1 `person_index` in step;
  - deactivate their WorkOS organization membership;
  - make sure the session check denies them through the DO (ADR 0010).

  Ended Memberships join the same DO check.
- **The permission function** `(person, action, resource)` lives in one place in `packages/domain` and is tested as a table (SPEC §5.6). Authorize from the Organization DO's data, not cached token claims, for admin actions.
- Team time zone: an IANA name, validated.
- The Organization setting for the minimum group size: N ≥ 3, default 4. Store it now. (Rounds store the N they closed under later, per ADR 0004.)
- Minimal web screens using the message catalog, Tailwind, and Base UI: Teams list, Team detail (members and Leads, add by email, remove), and Organization settings (N, admins).
